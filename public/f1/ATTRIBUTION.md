# RaceSide F1 Assets Attribution

## Madring 3D model

Derived model, downloaded 2026-09-07: Ayuntamiento de Madrid / Geoportal municipal
circuit CAD (2026-07-03), classified LiDAR and true orthophoto (flight 2026-06-13),
LoD2 buildings (2025-04-01). Modified for RaceSide; no municipal endorsement.
[Madrid reuse terms](https://datos.madrid.es/pages/condiciones-generales-ayuntamiento-de-madrid).
Elevation gap fill: IGN/CNIG, CC BY 4.0. Pit alignment/current footprints:
© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Full URLs, hashes, acquisition dates and
limitations: `tracks/3d/madring-metadata.json` and
`docs/track-model-madring-source-card.md` in the source repository.

## Baku 3D model

`tracks/3d/baku.glb` and `baku-preview.webp` combine the following sources:

- Planet Labs, SkySat image of Baku City Circuit, 9 April 2018: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Baku_City_Circuit,_April_9,_2018_SkySat.jpg — CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. The image was georegistered, cropped, resampled and applied to a terrain mesh. The derived visual assets are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The original image is not a 2026 survey.
- © OpenStreetMap contributors: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright — ODbL, Baku relation 11266687, mapped buildings and infrastructure. Geometry was projected to UTM 39N; unspecified building heights were estimated.
- GEDTM30 v1.1, OpenLandMap: https://github.com/openlandmap/GEDTM30/releases/tag/v1.1 — CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Approximate EGM2008 heights; urban road profiles were regularised to remove DEM waves. No vertical exaggeration.
- FIA Azerbaijan 2025 event notes: https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2025_azerbaijan_grand_prix_-_event_notes_-_circuit_map_pit_lane_quarantine_zone_and_red_zone.pdf — factual circuit, control-line and pit-layout reference.
- Baku City Circuit 2026 event catalogue: https://www.bakucitycircuit.com/buy-now — grandstand names and placement references; dimensions are modelling estimates.

The public `tracks/3d/baku-metadata.json` records source hashes, provenance, approximation limits and scene metrics. Reference WorldView tiles are not embedded or redistributed in the model.

Building texture additions: Sefer azeri, [Ismailiyye palace main façade, Baku, 2015](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ismailiyye_palace_main_fa%C3%A7ade,_Baku,_2015.jpg), 17 September 2015; Ludvig14, [Baku Maiden Tower 004 7736](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Baku_Maiden_Tower_004_7736.jpg), 19 April 2019. Both photographs are CC BY-SA 4.0. Images were cropped, resized, assembled into a texture atlas and mapped onto their corresponding OSM buildings. The tower uses a repeated masonry detail from its own photograph; unphotographed facades remain approximate. All building roofs use georeferenced pixels from the Planet image credited above.
